# Education and Household Income in Siddharthnagar District, Uttar Pradesh

## Project Overview

This project examines the relationship between educational attainment and household income in Siddharthnagar district, Uttar Pradesh. It uses data from the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) to study educational patterns, household income distribution, and rural–urban differences.

The district is compared with the complete Uttar Pradesh dataset. Since Siddharthnagar is included in the state dataset, the two groups are overlapping rather than independent.

## Objectives

- Examine educational attainment in Siddharthnagar.
- Analyse household income distribution.
- Compare district-level patterns with Uttar Pradesh.
- Explore differences between rural and urban households.

## Tools and Technologies

- R
- RStudio
- Excel
- Data visualisation using R

## Methodology

The analysis includes:

- Data cleaning and preparation
- Grouping observations by educational attainment
- Calculation of mean years of education
- Household-level income analysis
- Classification of household income into low, middle, and high groups using state-level income tertiles
- Rural–urban comparisons
- Summary tables and graphical visualisations

## Key Findings

- Average years of education were lower in Siddharthnagar than in the Uttar Pradesh dataset.
- Average and median household income were also lower in Siddharthnagar.
- A larger proportion of district households fell into the low-income group compared with the state-level distribution.
- Rural–urban differences were observed in both education and household income.

## Data Availability

The original PLFS dataset is not included in this repository. It is withheld to respect data access and redistribution restrictions. The analysis script is provided for transparency and reproducibility, subject to access to the original dataset.

## Limitations

This is a descriptive analysis and does not establish a causal relationship between education and household income. Missing observations and the limitations of the available survey data may affect the results.
